# Sell Me a Sasquatch — Rules

Formerly known as **Market for Lemons**.

An organized Markdown adaptation of the supplied official rule sheet, `PMG-CORE_Instructions_02JULY2026_Web.pdf` (2 July 2026 version), supplemented by card close-ups in the publisher's official instructional video and the user's physical-deck inventory. This document preserves the gameplay rules and verified card effects; it is not a verbatim transcription. Promotional material and illustrations are omitted.

- **Players:** 2–6
- **Ages:** 10+
- **Playing time:** About 20 minutes

## Contents

- 120 cards
- 1 Buyer Squatch
- 6 Playmats
- 28 Point Tokens

## Objective

Collect sets of Creatures and trade them in for Point Tokens. Avoid completing sets of Nasties, which let another player take cards or points from you.

Victory is checked **at the end of a turn**, after all completed sets have been traded in.

| Number of players | Point Tokens needed to win |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | 5 |
| 3–4 | 4 |
| 5–6 | 5 |

## Setup for 3–6 players

1. Shuffle the cards and deal **5 cards face down** to each player. Players may look at their own hands, but keep them secret.
2. Put the remaining cards face down in the middle of the table as the **Draw Pile**. Leave room beside it for a **Discard Pile**.
3. Put the Point Tokens in the middle of the table.
4. Give each player a Playmat. Assemble it according to the instructions on its back and place it in front of that player.
5. Leave space in front of each player for their **Collection**.
6. Choose a starting player and place the **Buyer Squatch** in front of them. That player is the first **Buyer**.

For two players, use the changes in [Two-player rules](#two-player-rules).

## Hands, deals, and Collections

Cards occupy three distinct places during play:

| Place | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Hand** | Your secret cards, used to build deals. |
| **Deal** | Cards offered on the table for the current turn. Deals begin partly hidden. |
| **Collection** | Your face-up cards, used to complete sets and play Thingamabobs. |

The following restrictions are essential:

- Cards from your **hand can only go into deals**.
- Cards from **deals can only go into Collections**.
- **Thingamabobs can only be played from Collections**, never directly from a hand or deal.

Follow the specific instructions on a Thingamabob when using its effect.

## Card types

### Creatures

Trade in a completed Creature set to earn **1 Point Token**. The set depends on the Creature's size:

| Creature size | Copies in deck | Cards required for a set | Reward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tiny | 20 | Any 4 Tiny Creatures | 1 Point Token |
| Medium | 25 | Any 3 Medium Creatures | 1 Point Token |
| Big | 16 | Any 2 Big Creatures | 1 Point Token |
| Giant | 4 | 1 Giant Creature | 1 Point Token |

**Creature total: 20 + 25 + 16 + 4 = 65 cards.**

Creatures within a size category do **not** need to have matching names. For example, any two Big Creatures make a set.

The **C.C. (Community Creations)** icon identifies cards suggested by the game's community.

### Nasties

Each Nasty set requires matching cards of the indicated type. Completing a set triggers its penalty when you trade it in:

| Nasty | Copies in deck | Cards required | Effect when traded in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Poison Pill Bug | 10 | 2 Poison Pill Bugs | The Buyer may steal **1 card** from your Collection and add it to their own. They may choose to steal nothing. |
| Loan Shark | 10 | 2 Loan Sharks | The Buyer may steal **up to 2 cards** from your Collection and add them to their own. They may take 2, 1, or 0 cards. |
| Trojan Horse | 12 | 3 Trojan Horses | The Buyer steals **1 Point Token** from you. If you have none, they receive nothing. |

**Nasty total: 10 + 10 + 12 = 32 cards.**

**Important timing:** In a game with 3–6 players, the Buyer Squatch changes hands before sets are traded in. The **new Buyer** resolves the Nasty penalties, including penalties suffered by the player who was Buyer at the beginning of the turn.

### Thingamabobs

Thingamabobs have special effects. They do not require sets: read each card for its effect.

You can only play them **from your Collection**, during the Thingamabob opportunity in a turn. Discard the Thingamabob face up, then resolve its instructions.

The user's physical-deck inventory contains these five Thingamabob types:

| Thingamabob | Copies in deck | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Platonic Isolator | 3 | Steal **1 Point Token** from any player who has **more Point Tokens than you**. |
| Super Detrital Repositioner | 3 | Remove **2 cards from 1 deal**, or **1 card each from 2 deals**, and put them in the Discard Pile. |
| Detrital Repositioner | 6 | Remove **1 card from 1 deal** and put it in the Discard Pile. |
| Spectroelectric Optimeter | 5 | Turn **1 card in any deal face up**. |
| Cryptozooptic Expander | 6 | Add **1 card from your hand, face down**, to any deal. |

**Thingamabob total: 3 + 3 + 6 + 5 + 6 = 23 cards.**

All five types can only be played **from your Collection**, following the normal Thingamabob timing and discard procedure.

## A turn with 3–6 players

### 1. Offer deals

The player with the Buyer Squatch is the Buyer.

Every **other player**:

1. Chooses **3 cards from their hand**.
2. Places those cards **face down** in front of their Playmat as their deal.
3. Turns **1 of those 3 cards face up**.

The Buyer does not offer a deal.

### 2. Reveal one additional card

Once all deals are ready, the Buyer turns over **1 additional card from one player's deal**.

This is one extra card total, not one extra card from every deal.

### 3. Allow Thingamabobs

After revealing the additional card, the Buyer asks whether anyone wants to play Thingamabobs.

- **Any player, including the Buyer**, may play Thingamabobs.
- Players may play **as many as they want**, provided the cards are in their Collections.
- To play one, place it face up in the Discard Pile and follow its instructions.
- Thingamabobs in hands or in deals cannot be played.

At the beginning of the game, Collections are empty, so there are no Thingamabobs available to play.

### 4. Choose a deal and distribute the cards

Before choosing, the Buyer announces that they are ready to choose a deal and gives everyone a **last chance to play Thingamabobs**.

After that opportunity:

1. The Buyer chooses **exactly one deal**.
2. The Buyer takes **all cards in that deal** and places them **face up in their Collection**.
3. The Buyer gives the **Buyer Squatch to the player whose deal they chose**.
4. Every player whose deal was **not chosen** takes all cards from their own deal and places them **face up in their own Collection**.

The player whose deal was chosen does not also receive those cards. Their deal goes to the outgoing Buyer, and they become the new Buyer.

An unchosen deal returns to its offerer's Collection, including any Nasties in it.

### 5. Trade in all completed sets

After the deal cards enter Collections, **all players must trade in all completed sets**. Trading in a completed set is mandatory; you cannot save it for a later turn.

Resolve the two categories in this order:

#### First: Nasties

1. Put completed Nasty sets face up in the Discard Pile.
2. Resolve the penalty printed on each set.
3. The **new Buyer** takes any cards or Point Tokens specified by those penalties.

Trade in completed Nasty sets **before** completed Creature sets. Cards taken by Nasty effects go into the Buyer's Collection; all completed sets must be resolved before the turn ends.

#### Second: Creatures

1. Put completed Creature sets face up in the Discard Pile.
2. Take **1 Point Token for each set** traded in.

### 6. Refill hands and end the turn

Once everyone has traded in **all completed sets**, all players draw until they have **5 cards in their hands**.

The turn is over. Check whether anyone has won. If the game continues, the player who now has the Buyer Squatch takes the next turn as Buyer.

Turn order therefore follows the chosen deals; it is not a fixed clockwise rotation.

## Running out of cards

If the Draw Pile runs out, shuffle the Discard Pile to make a new Draw Pile and continue playing.

## Winning and ties

At the **end of any turn**, a player wins if they have enough Point Tokens:

- **3–4 players:** 4 Point Tokens.
- **5–6 players:** 5 Point Tokens.
- **2 players:** 5 Point Tokens.

Reaching the target during a turn does not immediately end the game. Points can still be stolen before the turn ends.

If two or more players qualify to win at the same time, keep playing until one player both:

1. Has at least the required number of Point Tokens; and
2. Has **more Point Tokens than every other player**.

## Two-player rules

Use the normal card, Collection, set-trading, and Thingamabob rules, with the following changes.

### Setup

- Leave the **Buyer Squatch and Playmats** in the box.
- Deal **5 cards to each player**. Players may look at their own cards but keep them secret.
- Choose a player to take the first turn.
- Prepare the Draw Pile, Discard Pile, Point Tokens, and Collection spaces as usual.

### 1. Divide three cards into deals

On your turn, choose **3 cards from your hand** and place them **face down** on the table. You may distribute them in any of these ways:

| Cards in front of you | Cards in front of the other player |
| --- | --- |
| 3 | 0 |
| 2 | 1 |
| 1 | 2 |
| 0 | 3 |

Then choose **1 of the 3 cards** and turn it face up.

The other player **does not turn over an additional card**, unless they play a Thingamabob that allows it.

### 2. Allow Thingamabobs

Use the normal Thingamabob opportunity before the deal decision. Only Thingamabobs already in Collections can be played; follow their card instructions.

### 3. Accept or reverse the deal

The **other player** chooses one of two outcomes:

| Choice | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| **Accept the deal** | Each player takes the cards offered in front of themselves. |
| **Reverse the deal** | The players swap the offered deals: the other player takes the cards in front of you, and you take the cards in front of them. |

Both players put the cards they receive **face up in their Collections**. A deal may contain no cards, so one player may receive nothing.

### 4. Trade in sets

Both players must trade in all completed sets, resolving **Nasties before Creatures**.

Whenever you trade in a Nasty set, the **other player** decides which cards you lose, when applicable. The Nasty effects operate against you for that other player's benefit; there is no Buyer Squatch in this version.

### 5. Refill hands and alternate turns

At the end of each turn, make sure both players have **5 cards in their hands**.

Players **alternate turns**. The first player to have **5 Point Tokens at the end of a turn** wins. Apply the normal tie rule if both players qualify at the same time.

## Quick reference

### 3–6 players

1. Everyone except the Buyer offers 3 cards and reveals 1.
2. Buyer reveals 1 additional card from one deal.
3. Allow Thingamabobs from Collections, including a final opportunity before choosing.
4. Buyer takes one deal; its offerer becomes the new Buyer.
5. All other offerers take their own deals.
6. Everyone trades in all completed Nasties, then Creatures.
7. Everyone refills to 5 cards; check for a winner.

### 2 players

1. Active player divides 3 cards between the two offered deals and reveals 1 card.
2. Allow Thingamabobs from Collections.
3. Other player accepts or reverses the deals.
4. Both players add received cards to their Collections.
5. Trade in all completed Nasties, then Creatures.
6. Refill both hands to 5; check for a winner; alternate turns.
